FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is timesheet software?

Timesheet software records the hours each employee works in a period, sends them to a manager for approval, and keeps an accurate record for payroll and billing. FlexDays does this in one app on mobile and web, connected to leave, work schedules and a time tracker.

Is there a free timesheet app?

Yes. FlexDays is free for teams of up to 10 employees, with no time limit and no credit card, and every company gets its first 30 days free at any size. After that it's $2 per employee per month, and you only pay for employees beyond the first 10.

Can timesheets be weekly or monthly?

Both. Choose weekly periods that start on any weekday, or monthly periods that start on any day of the month. If you change it later, the new setting starts when the current period ends.

How does leave show up on a timesheet?

When a leave request is approved, those days are marked as leave on the timesheet and the expected hours drop to match. Half days and public holidays work the same way, so nobody has to adjust anything by hand.

Who approves timesheets?

Timesheets follow the approval chain assigned to each employee, with one or more levels. Approvers get a notification, approve or reject with a note, and the employee is told the decision. HR can also reassign an approver or settle a stuck timesheet.

Can I export timesheets for payroll?

Yes. Export timesheets for any date range to Excel or CSV, optionally filtered by status, and hand the file to payroll.

Does it work with a time tracker?

Yes. FlexDays has a built-in time tracker with a one-tap timer and clock in and out. Tracked time goes straight into the timesheet on the right project and day.
