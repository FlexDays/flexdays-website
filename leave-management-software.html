Frequently asked questions

What is leave management software?

Leave management software lets employees request time off, managers approve it, and HR track balances automatically — replacing spreadsheets and email chains. FlexDays does this in one simple app, on mobile and web, and connects leave directly to timesheets and working schedules.

Is FlexDays leave management free?

Yes. FlexDays is free for teams up to 10 employees with no time limit. Above that it is $2 per employee per month, the same price at any size, and you only pay for employees beyond the first 10.

Can it handle different leave types and multi-country holidays?

Yes. You can define annual, sick, WFH, and custom leave types, each with its own rules, and public holidays are applied automatically by country and location — ideal for teams that work across borders.

Does it work for large teams?

Yes. FlexDays scales from a handful of people to hundreds, with team-based multi-level approvals, HR analytics, and configurable schedules built for larger, distributed workforces.
