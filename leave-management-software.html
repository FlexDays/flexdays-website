FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is a leave management system?

A leave management system (also called leave management software or a leave management app) lets employees request time off, managers approve it, and HR track balances automatically, replacing spreadsheets and email chains. FlexDays does this in one app on mobile and web, connected to timesheets and schedules.

Is there a free leave management system?

Yes. FlexDays is a free leave management system for teams of up to 10 employees, with no time limit and no credit card. Every company also gets its first 30 days free at any size. After that it's $2 per employee per month, and you only pay for employees beyond the first 10.

Can it handle different leave types and multi-country holidays?

Yes. Annual, sick, WFH, and custom types each with their own rules, and public holidays applied automatically by country and location.

Does it work for large teams?

Yes. FlexDays scales to hundreds of employees with multi-level approvals, time reports, and configurable schedules.

Can we bring over our existing leave balances?

Yes. Download the balance template, add each employee's email and the days they have left for each leave type, and upload it. FlexDays previews every change before applying it, and if any row has a mistake nothing is changed.

What is the best leave tracker app for employees?

A good leave tracker app lets employees request time off from their phone in seconds, see their own balance, and get an answer quickly. FlexDays has native iOS and Android apps plus a web portal, with automatic balances, team approvals and public holidays built in.

Is FlexDays web-based, or do I need to download it?

Both work. Managers and HR can use the web-based portal in any browser with nothing to install, and employees can download the free FlexDays mobile app for iOS or Android. Everything stays in sync.

How do I track employee leave?

Set up your leave types and public holidays, add your employees, and let them request leave in the app. Managers approve with one tap, balances update automatically, and the team calendar shows who's off. In FlexDays, setup takes about five minutes.

Can I track sick leave and other leave types?

Yes. Annual leave, sick leave, work from home and any custom leave type each get their own rules and balances, so sick leave is tracked separately from vacation.
