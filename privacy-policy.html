For data protection purposes we are jointly the controllers of the personal data described in this policy, except where section 2 says otherwise.

This policy covers our website (flexdays.app), the FlexDays web portal, and the FlexDays apps for iOS and Android (together, "FlexDays").

2. Two roles: your employer's data and ours

FlexDays is a workplace app. A company (our customer) signs up and adds its employees. That creates two different situations, and the law treats them differently:

  • Employee data your employer puts into FlexDays (your profile, leave, timesheets and so on): your employer is the controller. It decides what is recorded and why. We are its processor: we handle that data only to provide FlexDays, on its instructions. Your employer's own privacy notice also applies to you.
  • Everything else, such as company administrator accounts, billing contacts, website visitors and waitlist subscribers: we are the controller.
If you are an employee and want to see, correct or delete your data, the quickest route is usually your employer's HR team or FlexDays administrator. We will help them, and you can always contact us directly too.

5. Who can see your data inside FlexDays

FlexDays shows each person only what their role allows. In general, your managers, approvers and your company's administrators can see your profile, leave and time records. On the shared calendar, colleagues in your team, department or whole company (depending on your company's settings) can see when you are on leave and the leave type. They never see the reason you entered: only you, the people who approve your request, and your company's administrators can. Announcements and events are shown to the people they were sent to. Your company's administrators decide these roles.

Each company's data is kept separate from every other company's at the database level, so one customer can never see another customer's data.

7. Where it is stored

We are based in Egypt. FlexDays and its database are hosted by Railway in the United States, and our other providers process data in their own locations, so your data is processed outside your own country. Where a transfer is subject to EU or UK data protection law, it relies on the safeguards our providers offer, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

8. How long we keep it

  • Company and employee data: for as long as the company's FlexDays account exists, so leave history and balances stay correct.
  • Offboarded employees: when an employer offboards someone, the person loses access straight away, but their records stay in the company's history (so past leave and timesheets still add up) until the employer asks us to delete them or the company account is closed.
  • Closed company accounts: when a customer closes its account and asks us to, we delete or anonymise its data within 90 days, unless the law requires us to keep part of it (for example billing records for tax).
  • Sign-in sessions: access tokens expire after 15 minutes, and sessions after at most 30 days. Signing out ends a session immediately.
  • Verification and password reset codes: minutes to an hour, then they stop working and are cleaned up.
  • Audit trail: for as long as the company account exists, because it is part of the record of who changed what.
  • Billing records: as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Waitlist emails: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.
  • Backups: kept by our hosting provider for a limited rolling period and then deleted automatically. Deleted data can remain in a backup until that period passes.

9. Cookies

Website (flexdays.app). We use the Meta Pixel, a small piece of code from Meta that uses cookies, to count visits and waitlist sign-ups that come from our ads, so we can improve them. Meta may combine this with information it holds about you under Meta's Privacy Policy. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or change your Meta ad preferences.

Web portal and apps. We use only what is needed to keep you signed in and the service working. There are no advertising or analytics cookies in the portal or the apps.

10. Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, restrict or object to how it is used, or receive it in a portable format. You can withdraw consent at any time where we rely on it.

For employee data, we act on your employer's instructions (section 2), so we may pass your request to your employer, and we will help them answer it. For data we control, email support@beanvoid.com. We respond within 30 days, free of charge. We may need to confirm your identity first, so that nobody can access or delete someone else's data.

These rights come from Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 151 of 2020) and, if you are in the EU or the UK, from the GDPR. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

11. Security

All traffic uses TLS (HTTPS), the service sits behind Cloudflare, each company's data is isolated at the database level, passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes, and repeated failed sign-ins lock an account. Our Security page explains the details.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach ever affects your data, we will tell the affected customers, and the relevant authority, as the law requires.

12. Children

FlexDays is a workplace tool and is not intended for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect children's data; if we learn that we have, we delete it.

13. Changes and contact

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top and tell company administrators by email or in the app. Questions, requests, or anything that looks wrong: support@beanvoid.com.
