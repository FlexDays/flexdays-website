Timesheets × Leave × Schedule

The trio that actually
works together.

Most HR tools treat timesheets and leave as separate systems. FlexDays connects them from day one, so your data is always consistent and your team never has to reconcile manually.

1

Admin configures the schedule once

Set working days, shift hours, and locations per team. Everything downstream uses this as the single source of truth.

2

Leave is deducted from the schedule automatically

Approved leave days are reflected immediately in timesheets. No manual adjustments, no reconciliation ever.

3

Balances stay accurate in real time

Leave balances update the moment a request is approved, cancelled, or modified, always live, always correct.

4

Managers see the full picture

Team schedules, leave overlaps, and working hours in one unified view. No switching between apps.
