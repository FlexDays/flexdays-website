What HR teams in Egypt should know

Holidays are fully paid

Official public holidays are paid days off and can't be deducted from an employee's annual leave balance.

Working on a holiday

If an employee works on a public holiday, they get their normal wage plus extra holiday pay, or a substitute day off if they ask for it in writing.

The Thursday rule

Since 2020 Egypt has often moved mid-week holidays (like 25 January, 30 June and 6 October) to Thursday. Plan rosters around the official decree, not the calendar date.

Coptic Easter

Coptic Easter Sunday (2 May 2027) is a day off for Christian employees. Sham El-Nessim the next day is a holiday for everyone.

Frequently asked questions

How many public holidays are there in Egypt in 2027?

Egypt has 13 public holiday occasions in 2027. Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha each last several days, so the total number of days off is higher.

When is Eid al-Fitr 2027 in Egypt?

Eid al-Fitr is expected around 9 to 11 March 2027, depending on the sighting of the Shawwal moon.

When is Sham El-Nessim 2027?

Sham El-Nessim falls on Monday 3 May 2027, the day after Coptic Easter.

Are holidays moved to Thursday in Egypt?

Often, yes. The government regularly moves mid-week holidays to Thursday to create long weekends. Each move is announced by decree shortly before the holiday.
