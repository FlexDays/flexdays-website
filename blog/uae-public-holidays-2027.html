What HR teams in UAE should know

Same holidays for public and private sectors

The UAE gives the same official public holidays to government and private-sector employees.

Holidays can move to the start or end of the week

The Cabinet can move a holiday to the beginning or end of the week to create a long weekend. The two Eids are the exception: they always stay on their Hijri dates.

Weekend is Saturday and Sunday

Since 2022 the official weekend is Saturday and Sunday, with a shorter working day on Friday for government employees. Many private companies follow the same pattern.

Working on a public holiday

Under the UAE Labour Law, an employee who works on a public holiday gets either another day off in return, or their normal pay for that day plus at least 50% of their basic wage.

Frequently asked questions

How many public holidays are there in the UAE in 2027?

The UAE has around 13 days of official public holidays in 2027, including Eid Al Fitr, Arafat Day, Eid Al Adha and the 2-day National Day holiday.

When is Eid Al Fitr 2027 in the UAE?

Eid Al Fitr is expected around Wednesday 10 March 2027. The holiday runs from 29 Ramadan to 3 Shawwal, expected around 9 to 12 March.

When is National Day 2027 in the UAE?

Eid Al Etihad (National Day) is on Thursday 2 and Friday 3 December 2027, giving a long weekend through Sunday 5 December.

Do private-sector employees get the same holidays in the UAE?

Yes. The UAE's official public holidays apply to both the public and private sectors.
