What HR teams in Philippines should know

Regular holidays: pay rules

If an employee doesn't work on a regular holiday, they still get 100% of their daily wage. If they work, they get 200% of their daily wage for the first 8 hours.

Special non-working days: pay rules

The rule is "no work, no pay" unless your company policy or a collective agreement says otherwise. Employees who work on a special non-working day get an extra 30% of their daily rate.

EDSA Day is a working day

In 2027, the EDSA People Power Revolution Anniversary (25 February) is a special working day, so it's a normal working day with normal pay.

Eid dates come later

Eid'l Fitr and Eid'l Adha are regular holidays, but their dates are proclaimed separately closer to the time. Expect them around 10 March and 16 May 2027.

Frequently asked questions

How many holidays are there in the Philippines in 2027?

Proclamation No. 1427 sets 10 regular holidays and 8 special non-working days for 2027, plus Eid'l Fitr and Eid'l Adha, which are proclaimed separately.

When is Holy Week 2027 in the Philippines?

Maundy Thursday is 25 March, Good Friday is 26 March and Black Saturday is 27 March 2027, giving a four-day break from Thursday to Sunday.

Is EDSA Day a holiday in 2027?

No. In 2027 the EDSA People Power Revolution Anniversary on 25 February is a special working day, so it's a normal working day.

When is National Heroes Day 2027?

National Heroes Day falls on Monday 30 August 2027, the last Monday of August, giving a long weekend.
