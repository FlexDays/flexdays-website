What HR teams in US should know

Federal holidays aren't mandatory for private employers

US federal law doesn't require private companies to give paid holidays. Most still observe the big ones (New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas), but the exact list is your company's choice. Write it down in your leave policy so everyone knows.

The weekend rule

When a federal holiday falls on a Saturday, the day off moves to the Friday before. When it falls on a Sunday, it moves to the Monday after. In 2027 this affects Juneteenth, Independence Day, Christmas and New Year's Day 2028.

Two New Year's Days in one year

Because January 1, 2028 is a Saturday, the observed day off is Friday, December 31, 2027. So 2027 effectively has 12 federal days off. Note that banks and markets may still open that day.

States add their own holidays

Many states add holidays such as Lincoln's Birthday, Cesar Chavez Day or the day after Thanksgiving. If your team works across several states, check each state's list.

Frequently asked questions

How many federal holidays are there in 2027?

There are 11 federal holidays in 2027. Because New Year's Day 2028 falls on a Saturday, it is observed on Friday, December 31, 2027, giving most federal employees 12 days off in calendar year 2027.

Do private companies have to give federal holidays off?

No. US federal law doesn't require private employers to give paid holidays. Most companies observe the major ones, but the list is set by company policy or employment contracts.

When is Juneteenth observed in 2027?

Juneteenth (June 19) falls on a Saturday in 2027, so the federal day off is Friday, June 18, 2027.

Is Christmas 2027 observed on Friday?

Yes. December 25, 2027 is a Saturday, so the federal holiday is observed on Friday, December 24, 2027.
